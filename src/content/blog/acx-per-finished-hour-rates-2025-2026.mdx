## PFH is only the starting point

Narrators often compare deals with PFH alone, but the better comparison is PFH plus timeline risk, rights type, and expected admin overhead.

## The benchmark habit

Set one benchmark line for your current minimum acceptable PFH rate. Then compare every accepted and declined offer against that line over time.
